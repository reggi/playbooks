# OpenSCAD and 3D printing

**Status: Do not use yet.**

This playbook will define how parametric 3D-printing models are designed and delivered with OpenSCAD.

Topics to specify:

- units, tolerances, wall thicknesses, and clearances;
- parameter naming and model organization;
- print orientation and support avoidance;
- preview, render, and export workflow;
- printer and material assumptions;
- expected source, STL, and documentation outputs.
